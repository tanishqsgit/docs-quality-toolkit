# Review a task-based topic

- Can the intended reader identify the outcome from the title and opening sentence?
- Are version, permissions, dependencies, and required inputs explicit?
- Does each numbered step describe an action with the exact command or UI label?
- Are expected results observable, including a way to confirm completion?
- Do examples use synthetic data and explain placeholders?
- Are destructive actions explained before the action?
- Can the reader recover from the most likely failure?
- Are terms consistent, sentences direct, and links relevant?
- Has a reviewer followed the procedure against the stated product version?
- Are AI-assisted claims grounded in evidence, with unresolved points removed or labelled?
- Has the responsible writer approved publication separately from automated checks?

This is an independent portfolio checklist, not a company style guide.
